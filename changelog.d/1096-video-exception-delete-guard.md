- An unexpected failure during video analysis no longer counts as Frigate having lost the clip. With the legacy `MAINTENANCE__AUTO_DELETE_MISSING_CLIPS` setting on, such a failure applied the missing-media policy: under **Delete records YA-WAMF has no photo for** it could delete the record and its counted birds, so **Analyse all unknowns** could lower the day's captures. Only Frigate reporting the event or clip as gone now applies that policy (#1096).
- A visit renamed to a domestic bird you keep moves to Garden only while its name is still unconfirmed: if you confirm the name at the same moment, the record stays in bird history and nothing is written to Garden (#1096).
- **Analyse all unknowns** now explains that naming a capture can join it to a neighbouring visit of the same species on the same camera, so the visit count may fall while every capture is kept (#1096).
