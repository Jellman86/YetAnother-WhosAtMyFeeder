- A guest's live connection now tells a removal apart from an arrival. When you hide or delete a record, or an automatic false-positive check removes one, an open guest page is told at once that something left the shared history, while a new capture or a record shown again still arrives as an ordinary change. The message carries no ID or any other detail, and owners' pages are unchanged. Guest pages look the same in this release; it is the first step towards guests no longer seeing a removed record for a few seconds after you hide it. See [the API reference](docs/api.md) (#1121).
