- The first-run setup wizard draws from the app's colour tokens in light, dark and high contrast,
  and its connection check marks a passed or failed test with the same shaped status icons as the
  rest of the app.
- On a phone or small tablet held sideways (for example 844 × 390 or 1024 × 600) the wizard
  tightens its header and footer and drops the step's decorative icon, so the step itself has
  more room while Back, Skip and Continue stay in view. The "Skip setup?" prompt keeps its two
  buttons in place and scrolls only its words, so it never pushes the step's actions off the
  card.
