- A species or Garden animal is only ever described by an article about it. A Garden record of an
  Eastern Gray Squirrel showed Wikipedia's summary of a mite, because the mite's article lists the
  squirrel among its hosts and any mention of the scientific name counted as a match. An article
  now has to be titled with the scientific name or name it in its opening sentence; anything else
  is rejected before it is cached, and the record shows no description rather than a wrong one.
  Birds looked up by name are checked the same way whenever the taxonomy knows their scientific
  name.
- A record and its family tree use one name for the species. A record headed "European Starling"
  no longer ends its family tree, or titles the tree dialog, with the catalogue's "Common
  Starling"; other ranks keep the catalogue's names.
