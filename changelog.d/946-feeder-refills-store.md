- YA-WAMF can now keep a record of when you refill a feeder, so a jump in visits afterwards has an explanation beside it. This release adds the store and an owner-only API: `POST /api/refills` records a refill now or at a time you give, for one camera or none, with an optional note; `GET /api/refills` lists a window of them; `DELETE /api/refills/{id}` removes one for good. The same automation event sent twice, or two refills of one camera within 15 minutes, is recorded once. Refills sit in their own table and never change your detection history; guests cannot see them. Markers on the Leaderboard timeline and a **Record a refill** button follow. See [the API reference](docs/api.md#feeder-refills) (#946).
