- A record now tells you when its snapshot and its video named different birds. When the video reading finishes, YA-WAMF compares it with the snapshot reading by catalogue species, not by label text, and on a disagreement the record shows both readings with their scores under **Snapshot and video**, marked **Readings disagree**, so you can look at the photo and the clip and confirm the name or pick another. While the record still has the snapshot's name, **Score again** is offered beside them; this replaces the old "Video found a match: confirm to apply" notice, which blamed the minimum confidence floor even when the floor was not the reason. Nothing is renamed by the check, and once you name the bird the readings stay as evidence without the warning. A reading below the naming floor, a bird one of the models cannot name, or a name borrowed from Frigate's sub-label is never counted as a disagreement. Visits analysed before this release are not compared. This is the first step towards [comparing two models](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/804); see [Deep Video Analysis](docs/features/video-analysis.md#when-the-snapshot-and-the-video-disagree) and [the API reference](docs/api.md) (#804).
