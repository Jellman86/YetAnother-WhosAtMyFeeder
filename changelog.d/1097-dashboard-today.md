- **The Dashboard has a Today window.** Beside the heading, choose **Today** to see what has
  visited since your local midnight, or **Last 24 hours** (still the default, so an early morning is
  never empty). Each device remembers its choice. Every figure, the field log, Garden visits, the
  activity chart, the Heard card, the species checks and **Needs your call** count the same window,
  so the waiting captures agree with the **Unresolved** figure. A new species still waits for you
  whenever it was first seen in the last fortnight. The field log's earlier-visits line opens the
  Explorer on the same window, and on Today it reads "earlier visits today". While the new window
  is read, nothing from the old one is shown under its name, and an open Dashboard reads the new day
  at midnight. When a guest's shared history starts after their midnight, the Dashboard says from
  what time Today is shown.
- **Visits after midnight land in the right hour in every time zone.** In zones a half or three
  quarters of an hour off UTC, such as India or Newfoundland, the day summary's hourly counts filed
  a capture just after midnight under 23:00 the day before. Each capture is now counted in the local
  hour it was made in.
- **The Explorer's periods match the rest of the app.** **Week** and **Month** are now **Last 7
  days** and **Last 30 days**, and they cover exactly 7 and 30 calendar days including today. They
  used to cover 8 and 31. Links that use `date=week` and `date=month` still work.
