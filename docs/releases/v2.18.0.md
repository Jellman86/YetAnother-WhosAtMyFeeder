# YA-WAMF 2.18.0

YA-WAMF 2.18.0 gives your feeder its own species catalogue, so bird names appear in your language
without an internet connection or an account. The dashboard has been rebuilt around the day's
activity, and the stalls that made the interface slow to load are fixed.

## What's new

- **Bird names in your language, offline.** YA-WAMF ships a species catalogue with 11,276 species
  and their translated names, built into the image and copied into `/data` on first start. Names no
  longer depend on reaching iNaturalist, and no account is needed. **Settings > Health** says where
  each name came from.
- **A dashboard built around your day.** The dashboard is now a field desk: one chronological log of
  the day, with repeat frames of the same bird folded into a single visit, and anything that needs
  your attention stated in words at the top. **Work through the queue** opens a walk-through rather
  than sending you to the Explorer.
- **An Explorer you can filter without scrolling.** Five rows of controls are now one filter bar with
  counted facets, so every filter says how many results it would return before you pick it. You can
  also switch the Explorer to a compact list under **Settings > Appearance**, or with the
  **Cards / List** buttons on the page itself.
- **Choose how times are shown.** **Settings > Appearance** now offers your locale's clock, 12-hour,
  or 24-hour, and every time in the interface follows it.
- **Older Intel graphics work again.** The `full` and `intel` images now include Intel's legacy
  OpenCL and Level Zero packages alongside the modern driver stack, restoring OpenVINO on Gen8, Gen9,
  and Gen11 hardware such as Coffee Lake.
- **Remove a model you no longer use.** Installed models can be deleted from **Settings >
  Detection**, so an unused download stops taking up disk.

## Smoother everyday use

- **The interface no longer stalls while the server is busy.** Requests could queue behind database
  work that held a connection open across a slow call to Frigate, which made pages slow to load and
  sometimes stopped them loading at all. Connections are now released before that outside work
  starts. Measured against a copy of a real installation, with twenty-four requests at once, the
  median request went from 2.3 seconds to 0.2, and the longest wait for a connection went from 4.7
  seconds to 0.18. A saturated pool now answers honestly instead of hanging, and two
  reclassifications running at once can no longer deadlock the server.
- **Your history stops claiming Frigate still holds a deleted clip.** Detections kept reporting an
  event that Frigate retired days ago. A bounded scan can now reconcile this, and a detection Frigate
  withdraws as a false positive is taken out of view properly.
- **A bird you have renamed stays renamed, and stops counting twice.** Your own common name is
  recorded against the species in the catalogue, so it survives a taxonomy refresh and the
  leaderboard counts one bird rather than two.
- **Times read to the minute everywhere.** A detection showed `07:38` in one place and `07:38:00` in
  another. Twenty-four places carried the stray seconds, including the eBird sightings on the
  dashboard, where second-precise timestamps on somebody else's report were precision nobody
  recorded.
- **Clearer colour and clearer controls.** Amber now means one thing: something needs a person.
  Success and "needs your attention" are no longer the same colour, collapsible sections say whether
  they are open, and keyboard focus is visible on every control.
- **Portuguese installations get Portuguese bird names from eBird.** They were getting English.

## Before you update

- **Back up `/config` and `/data`, then update normally.** This release adds seven database
  migrations across two databases and creates a new `/data/species_catalog.db`. YA-WAMF applies them
  during startup; keep the container running until the startup view reports the backend is ready.
- **Expect a short catalogue backfill on the first start.** Detections whose recorded scientific name
  matches exactly one catalogue entry gain a catalogue identity. This runs in the background and does
  not block startup. A name that is ambiguous, or that the catalogue does not carry, is left exactly
  as it is and counted in **Settings > Health**.
- **If your catalogue file goes missing, YA-WAMF reports it instead of replacing it.** A lost
  catalogue may have held your own renames, so it is left for you rather than quietly overwritten
  with the shipped seed.
- **Keep the unsuffixed tag unless you intentionally use a smaller runtime image.** `latest` and
  `v2.18.0` remain the full compatibility build. Read the
  [hardware-acceleration guide](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.18.0/docs/setup/hardware-acceleration.md)
  before switching to `v2.18.0-cpu`, `v2.18.0-intel`, or `v2.18.0-cuda`; change only the image tag so
  the existing `/config`, `/data`, models, and provider preference stay intact.

## Good to know

- **The Explorer list view is off by default.** Cards remain the default. Turn the list on under
  **Settings > Appearance** for the whole installation, or use the **Cards / List** buttons to change
  it on the device you are holding.
- **The media integrity scan is off by default, and it is renamed.** `auto_purge_missing_clips` and
  `auto_purge_missing_snapshots` are now `media_integrity_scan_enabled` and
  `media_integrity_scan_media`. The old names still work. The old names were also misleading: with
  the default behaviour the scan marks a detection, it does not delete anything. Turn it on if you
  want `frigate_status` kept accurate as Frigate retention expires.
- **Catalogue identity is assigned conservatively.** A row is only given an identity when its
  scientific name resolves to exactly one catalogue concept or recorded synonym. Nothing else is
  written, name snapshots are never touched, and re-running changes nothing.
- **Anonymous telemetry stays opt-in, with a stated boundary.** Heartbeats retain only a hashed
  installation identity, cohorts below three installations are hidden from public figures, and
  turning both telemetry options off requests deletion before rotating the local identity.
