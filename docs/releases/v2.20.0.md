A release for anyone who has starred a visit and later found its clip gone, and for anyone who
likes looking at their birds. A favourite is now kept for good, the About page and the
leaderboard open on your own photographs, and choosing the right frame of a visit is one strip
and one click.

## What's new

- **A favourite is kept for good.** Pressing the star now copies the visit's photograph and, when
  Frigate still has it, its clip into `/config/archive`, where no cache cleanup, cache clear or
  Frigate rotation reaches. The star says what it holds: archiving, archived with its size, photo
  only when Frigate had no clip, or failed with a retry. Favourites you already have are archived
  on the first start after updating. Only unfavouriting, deleting the visit, **Remove all
  favourites** or a database reset remove an archive, and each of those now says so.
- **Keep the newest of each species.** A per-species floor under **Settings → Data** keeps the
  newest N visits of every species, and their photographs, through age cleanup. Off by default;
  clips are not held.
- **The About page opens on your feeder's own photographs.** Two rows of recent captures, one
  crop per species, drift across the top of the page and pause under the pointer; each card opens
  the visit. Beside the stats, how many installs reported to the telemetry service this week; it
  follows your update-check opt-out and is absent rather than zero when unknown.
- **The leaderboard opens on a showcase of its species.** The leader stands expanded with its
  count, trend, confidence and last visit, the next eight species beside it as tiles, and clicking
  a tile brings it forward. Every tile is this feeder's own newest crop of that species; where
  there is none, the species' reference image stands in and is labelled as a reference photo, not
  as yours.
- **One strip to choose the frame and settle the species.** The detection record's photograph
  has one strip of the visit's moments beneath it, in time order. Hovering or focusing a thumbnail
  shows the frame at decision size with what the model read in it, and **Use this frame** changes
  the photograph and nothing else. The Best crop / Full frame switch is gone: the photograph is
  the crop, a hover peeks at the whole scene with the crop outlined, and only then is using the
  whole scene offered. The actions read **Confirm**, **Pick a different species** and **Score
  again**. The review queue works the same way and now shows every frame kept from a visit.

## Smoother everyday use

- **A restarted video worker no longer costs you the visit** (carried from 2.19.7). Queued work
  waits for the replacement, a visit whose worker was lost is queued again, and the video worker
  is judged on a thirty-second heartbeat budget that fits a long analysis.
- **The record works on a phone.** A tap on a frame thumbnail opens its sheet instead of
  flickering, the frame comparison opens at the foot of the screen with its own Close, and the
  actions stack full width.

## Before you update

- **A schema migration runs on first start.** The favourites table gains the archive's state
  columns. It is automatic and reversible, but back up `/data/speciesid.db` first, as for any
  release with a migration.
- **`/config/archive` must be on your persistent volume.** It sits beside `/config/media_cache`,
  so a standard `/config` mount needs nothing. On first start every existing favourite is
  archived, fetching its clip from Frigate where one still exists, so expect disk use to grow by
  roughly one clip per favourite and the pass to take a while on a large collection. Back up the
  database and `/config/archive` together from now on.
- Unsuffixed image tags remain the full compatibility image; see
  [hardware acceleration](../setup/hardware-acceleration.md) if you use a provider-specific
  flavour.

## Good to know

- **Two new settings, both optional.** `MEDIA_CACHE__PER_SPECIES_MINIMUM` (default `0`, off) is the
  per-species floor; `CLASSIFICATION__VIDEO_WORKER_HEARTBEAT_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (default `30`) is the
  video worker's heartbeat budget. **Settings → Data** shows the archive's size apart from the
  cache, with how many archives failed and a retry.
- **The About page's install count leaves nothing new from your network.** It is read from the
  telemetry service's public summary, listed under "What leaves your network", and switched off
  with the update check.
