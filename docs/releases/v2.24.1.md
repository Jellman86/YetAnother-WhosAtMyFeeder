YA-WAMF 2.24.1 gives external services and notifications their own homes in Settings, keeps the
Leaderboard and species list counts intact after old captures are cleaned up, and restores Home
Assistant Marketplace (HACS) updates and Unraid Community Applications template validation.

## What's new

- **Dedicated Integrations and Notifications sections in Settings.** Settings now has seven
  sections: **Cameras & connection**, **Identifying birds**, **Integrations**, **Notifications**,
  **Access & privacy**, **Storage & upkeep** and **Display**. Every external service (BirdNET-Go,
  Home Assistant weather, eBird, iNaturalist, BirdWeather and the AI provider) sits on the first
  screen of **Integrations** with its own switch, its status in plain words, and its connection
  test. Your feeder's location now lives in **Cameras & connection** right after your cameras.
  Old links and bookmarks such as `/settings/alerts` and `/settings/sharing#integrations` still
  open the right place. See
  [How Settings is laid out](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.1/docs/setup/configuration.md#how-settings-is-laid-out).
- **Photo switches and custom rules in Notifications.** Discord, Pushover and Telegram each have a
  **Send the photo** switch in **Settings → Notifications**, so sending text-only alerts no longer
  requires an environment variable. **Custom rules** is now a fifth choice in the delivery
  sentence, with switches for when a visit starts, a later update if no alert has gone yet, and
  waiting for the video check. See
  [Notification Modes](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.1/docs/features/notifications.md#notification-modes).
- **Filter Audio History's activity grid by species.** Above **Calls heard by hour and weekday**,
  Audio History now shows the same species chips as the Leaderboard (**All species** plus the five
  most-heard species in the current window), so you can see when a single species calls across the
  week.
- **Quick access to Add observation from the header and Explorer.** **Add observation** moved out
  of the sidebar into the top-right controls next to Refresh, and desktop **Explorer** also shows a
  labelled **Add observation** button. The active page icon in the header is highlighted, and on a
  phone **Add observation** stays in the menu.

## Smoother everyday use

- **The Leaderboard and species list keep their counts after cleanup.** Ranked day, week and month
  boards and the all-time species list now read sealed days from saved daily totals, matching the
  charts, without counting kept favourites twice. See
  [Daily totals keep the counts](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.1/docs/features/favourites.md#daily-totals-keep-the-counts).
- **API scripts and Home Assistant get hours in your time zone.** Requests without an `X-Timezone`
  header now use the container's `TZ` for hourly summaries, timelines, activity heatmaps, species
  charts and custom backfills instead of falling back to UTC. See
  [Calendar days and `today`](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.1/docs/api.md#calendar-days-and-today).
- **Home Assistant Marketplace (HACS) updates work again.** Added the root `hacs.json` manifest
  required for HACS and Home Assistant Marketplace installations and updates from release tags
  ([#1067](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/1067)).
- **Unraid Community Applications template validation is clean.** Removed a stray static
  `apps/ui/public/sitemap.xml` that caused Community Applications to report a template read error
  ([#1054](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/1054)), and added a
  contract test so only Unraid's XML files exist in the repository.
- **Settings backups without a model keep the bundled starter model.** Importing a backup that does
  not name `classification.model` now falls back to the bundled MobileNet V2 instead of an
  uninstalled model.
- **No missing menu at 767 to 768 px zoom.** The narrow header menu and docked sidebar now switch
  at the exact same breakpoint.

## Before you update

- **No special steps.** This release has no database migrations and requires no configuration
  changes. Pull the new image and restart the container as usual.
- **Keep your image family unless you mean to change it.** Unsuffixed tags remain the full
  compatibility image; see
  [Hardware Acceleration](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.1/docs/setup/hardware-acceleration.md)
  for optional CPU, Intel and CUDA image tags.

## Good to know

- **Integration test results last for your current visit.** Running a connection test on an
  integration card now stays visible when you switch to another Settings section and return, until
  you edit a value it ran with or reload the page. Results are still not stored across reloads.
- **Not in this release.** Running several models together
  ([#804](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/804)) and feeder refill
  markers ([#946](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/946)) remain under
  review.
