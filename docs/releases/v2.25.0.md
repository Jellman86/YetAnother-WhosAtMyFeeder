YA-WAMF 2.25.0 adds a **Today** window to the Dashboard, the Leaderboard and Audio history,
makes the Explorer's filter counts match what each filter shows, and keeps the photos you
confirmed or chose when storage limits clear old media.

## What's new

- **See today since midnight.** The Dashboard, the Leaderboard and Audio history each have a
  **Today** choice, counted from your local midnight. Every figure on a page counts the same
  window. An open Dashboard or Audio history moves to the new day after midnight, and a
  Leaderboard loaded across midnight reads again rather than mixing two days. The Dashboard
  still opens on **Last 24 hours** unless you choose **Today**, and each device remembers its
  choice.
- **The same period names everywhere.** Periods now read **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**,
  **Last 30 days** and so on across the app. The Leaderboard's Day, Week, Month and Total are now
  **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** and **All time**. The Explorer's **Last 7
  days** and **Last 30 days** now cover exactly 7 and 30 days including today, where Week and
  Month covered 8 and 31. Older links still open.
- **Explorer filter counts match their results.** The number beside each species, camera and
  filter now counts what choosing it would show, for the chosen period and for **Visits** or
  **Captures** under **Show**. While the numbers are read the list says **Counting…**.
- **Find the visits you confirmed.** Owners have a new **Owner confirmed** choice under **Only**
  in the Explorer. It shows the visits and captures where you named the species. Guests never
  see it.
- **Photos you confirmed or chose are kept.** When the storage limit, the per-species limit or
  age cleanup removes media, a capture you confirmed, or one whose photo you chose, now keeps its
  main photograph. Its clips, previews and other photo choices can still go.
- **A renamed BirdNET-Go source stays one source.** Renaming an audio source in BirdNET-Go no
  longer adds a second entry under **Audio history → Sources**. The source keeps all of its calls
  under its newest name, and the update matches the calls you already have to their source.

## Smoother everyday use

- **Visits can fall while Unknown birds are named, and the Dashboard now says why.** Under
  **Visits**, the Dashboard shows how many of the visits are Unknown birds. Hover over, focus or
  tap that count for the reason: once an Unknown bird is named, it can join a visit of the same
  species on the same camera, so **Visits** falls while every capture stays.
- **Video analysis no longer deletes a record because it failed.** With the legacy
  `MAINTENANCE__AUTO_DELETE_MISSING_CLIPS` setting on and **Delete records YA-WAMF has no photo
  for** chosen, a video analysis that failed or could not read its clip could delete the record,
  so **Analyse all unknowns** could lower the day's captures. Only Frigate reporting the event as
  gone or the clip as not retained now does that, and never to a record you added by hand.
- **Photo choices go in one click.** Removing a photo choice now removes every framing of that
  frame at once, and **Undo** brings back exactly what you removed. **As Frigate recorded it** can
  now be removed too when it is not the photograph in use.
- **Leaderboard thumbnails stay filled.** After **Show more visits**, visits whose cached photo was
  cleared by storage limits now show the photo Frigate still holds instead of a blank tile.
- **Each visit is named by its own species.** An Explorer visit whose capture also caught another
  species keeps its own name, with a note such as **Also in one capture: Common Wood-Pigeon**. In
  the record, **Full visit** is now **Full clip**, since it is the recording around that one
  capture, and the **Full clips** setting works as before.
- **The same controls wherever a record opens.** A record opened from About, the Dashboard or the
  Explorer now offers the same play button and full clip. Guests see a play button only while
  **Share video with guests** is on.
- **Steadier Health and status.** Guests and owners see the same **Unresolved** count. A GPU or
  NPU shows **Not measured** until its first reading, instead of being missing. A paused Frigate
  update retry is listed under **Skipped on purpose** rather than turning Health **Degraded**.
- **Custom notification rules are kept.** Under **Settings → Notifications**, switching from
  **custom rules** to another choice and back now restores the triggers you set.

## Before you update

- **Back up `/config` and `/data` first.** This release has one database migration. It adds
  source columns to your stored BirdNET-Go calls and copies each call's source id from the message
  it arrived with. It runs once while YA-WAMF starts, before anything else is written. No action
  is needed. Rolling back to an older version is covered by the
  [upgrade and rollback guide](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.25.0/MIGRATION.md#roll-back-safely).
- **Photo protection needs no migration.** It applies to existing captures from the next
  cleanup. A photo removed before this update cannot be brought back once Frigate no longer has it.
- **Keep your image family unless you mean to change it.** Unsuffixed tags remain the full
  compatibility image; see
  [Hardware Acceleration](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.25.0/docs/setup/hardware-acceleration.md)
  for optional CPU, Intel and CUDA image tags.

## Good to know

- **After renaming a source in BirdNET-Go, update its camera mapping** under **Settings →
  Integrations → BirdNET-Go → Audio buffer & sensor mapping**. Audio history follows the rename,
  but a camera mapped by the old name does not yet
  ([details](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.25.0/docs/integrations/birdnet-go.md#renaming-a-source-in-birdnet-go)).
