YA-WAMF 2.21.1 makes backfill summaries easier to trust when you replay older camera events.

## Smoother everyday use

- **See what actually grew your history.** New now counts detections added to your database. Updated counts existing detections whose automatic classification improved. Settings, job progress and the API show both. No saved detection is changed by this reporting fix.

## Before you update

- **No special steps.** Back up your data and configuration as usual, then wait for the container health check to pass. This patch has no database migration or required setting change.
- Unsuffixed image tags remain the full compatibility image. See the [provider-family guidance](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.21.1/docs/setup/hardware-acceleration.md) before choosing an optional CPU, Intel or CUDA image.

## Good to know

- Earlier backfill summaries cannot be split into New and Updated after the fact. Your saved detections are intact, and rerunning a range will not recreate its original counts. Future jobs report both numbers accurately.
- The [EVA02 Large / Intel GPU sustained-backfill warning](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/releases/tag/v2.21.0) still applies. A validated Intel NPU may be a better fit where supported. [Issue #490](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/490) remains open for the reporter's retest.
