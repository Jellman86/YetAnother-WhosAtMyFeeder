YA-WAMF 2.24.0 makes your feeder's history easier to read and to trust. Settings is organised by
what you came to do and has a search, the record shows the bird and the decision side by side, the
Leaderboard keeps its counts after cleanup removes old photos, and you can choose how far apart two
captures can be and still count as one visit. Status and Health now say only what was measured, and
the release fixes several Safari and iPhone layout problems.

## What's new

- **Find any setting, and read Settings at a glance.** Settings now has six sections in place of
  twelve tabs: **Cameras & connection**, **Identifying birds**, **Alerts**,
  **Sharing & integrations**, **Storage & upkeep** and **Display**. Each opens with what most people
  need, and the rest waits under **More options**. A **Search settings** box finds a setting by its
  name, what it does or a word such as "password", "GPU" or "backup", in your language, and opens it
  for you. Press **/** on Settings to reach it. Every setting is still there, and old links and
  bookmarks such as `/settings/models` still open the right place. See
  [How Settings is laid out](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.0/docs/setup/configuration.md#how-settings-is-laid-out).
- **Your Leaderboard keeps its history after cleanup.** Before cleanup removes a day's captures, it
  now saves how many captures and visits each species had that day. The Leaderboard's chart and each
  species' weekday and month charts count those days from the saved totals, so seasonal trends
  reach back past your retention period. The photos and clips for those days are still deleted, and
  the charts say from which day their counts come from daily totals. Totals start when you update.
  See [Daily totals](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.0/docs/features/favourites.md#daily-totals-keep-the-counts).
- **Choose how long a gap still counts as one visit.** On a busy feeder, birds of one species that
  come back every few seconds used to merge into one long visit. Set the gap under
  **Settings → Identifying birds → Counting visits**, from 5 to 300 seconds. It stays at 60 unless
  you change it. The Dashboard, Explorer, Leaderboard, statistics and Garden visits all follow it
  from the next page you open, with no restart. A shorter gap gives you more, shorter visits, but it
  cannot count individual birds.
- **The record shows the bird and the decision together.** On a laptop or desktop the photograph,
  the name, the score and **Confirm** sit in one column, with the family tree, the description and
  nearby sightings in a reading column beside them. A tall crop is shown whole, no longer between
  black bars. Rarely used actions such as **Hide** and **Delete this visit permanently** wait
  under **More actions**. On a phone the identification comes straight after the photograph.
- **System status never disappears.** Where the sidebar's **System status** card does not fit, a
  one-line summary takes its place, such as "2 issues need attention", linking to Health. Each state
  is said in words with a shaped dot, so it never depends on colour alone, and amber appears only
  when something needs you. On the Health chart, the CPU and each accelerator keep their own colour
  in every theme.
- **Audio history reads like the Leaderboard.** Calls heard are shown by weekday and hour in the
  Leaderboard's activity grid, in your own time zone, with the same window bar, figures and chart
  layout. The busiest hour is now your local hour; before, it was the hour in UTC.

## Smoother everyday use

- **A Garden bird that sat still keeps its photo.** Frigate often throws away an event whose object
  never moves, snapshot and all, so a chicken moved to Garden used to read "Photo expired". Garden
  now keeps YA-WAMF's own copy of the frame its bird model classified when Frigate has no snapshot.
  The clip is still Frigate's, and the capture says so when Frigate kept none.
- **Safari and iPhone behave properly.** With Reduce Motion on, a new **Text size** now reaches
  every word in Safari straight away, without a reload. The record's photograph fills its frame,
  notification rows keep their words inside the row at large text sizes, and leaving YA-WAMF in
  Safari goes where you asked, without a surprise reload. Scrollbars on a Mac hide when idle again.
- **Health no longer fails on a busy feeder.** `/health` and the diagnostics page could answer with
  an error while detections were arriving. That is fixed, and your detections were never affected.
- **Health raises fewer false alarms.** A snapshot Frigate deliberately discarded, a short Frigate restart or
  a single recovered GPU decode no longer shows as a problem. You can **Dismiss** a warning you have
  read; it comes back if it happens again. Health also names the setting that really dropped an
  event, such as the minimum confidence floor.
- **Tests tell the truth.** A Discord, Telegram or Pushover test now passes only when the channel
  accepted the message, and otherwise says why and what to check. Model and connection states read
  the same everywhere and never call a model running when its files are missing.
- **Record the light level with each detection, if you want to.** Name a lux sensor as the
  **Illuminance entity** under **Settings → Sharing & integrations → More options → Home Assistant
  weather**, and each new detection keeps its reading in the record's **Conditions** row and in the
  API. It is only ever a measurement, never an estimate. See
  [Home Assistant](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.0/docs/integrations/home-assistant.md#weather-and-light-from-your-own-sensors).
- **The Intel GPU reading stays right on the xe driver.** It no longer sinks towards zero after a
  while when the GPU is busy.
- **Less work for your server.** A quiet page makes far fewer requests, pages open with a few files
  instead of dozens, and on the Intel image Garden motion photos are decoded on the GPU, using about
  a third of the CPU they did.
- **Easier API scripts.** `/api/events` and `/api/visits` accept `start_date=today`, one
  `/api/visits` request returns up to 500 visits, and dates are calendar days in your time zone. See
  [Calendar days](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.0/docs/api.md#calendar-days-and-today).

## Before you update

- **Back up `/config` and `/data` first.** This update applies four database migrations to your
  detection history on startup: the daily totals, the light reading, a flag on cached species
  descriptions, and a new photo source for Garden. The species catalogue is unchanged. No settings
  need changing. Wait for the container health check to pass after the update.
- **Going back to v2.23.2 loses the new values.** A downgrade keeps your detections, but it deletes
  the daily totals, which are the only copy of the counts for days cleanup has already removed. It
  also drops the stored light readings, and relabels Garden's kept frames as Frigate snapshots.
  Updating again restores the tables, not those values. Changing only the tag is not a rollback;
  see the
  [upgrade and rollback guide](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.0/MIGRATION.md).
- **Check the browser on a wall tablet.** The dashboard now needs Chrome or Edge 111, Firefox 128,
  or Safari 16.4 or newer. On an iPhone or iPad that means iOS or iPadOS 16.4. An older device, such
  as an iPad that stopped at iPadOS 15, still opens the page but draws colours, borders and focus
  rings wrongly. See
  [supported browsers](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.0/docs/setup/getting-started.md#supported-browsers).
- **Check scripts that read the API or `/health`.** A date you pass is now a calendar day in your
  time zone rather than a UTC day, so a bird at 23:30 is no longer counted on the next day.
  `/health` now reports `degraded` while the MQTT broker is disconnected or Frigate does not answer;
  it still answers with HTTP 200. A settings save that is refused answers `422` with the field
  named, where a missing address scheme used to answer `400`.
- **Keep your image family unless you mean to change it.** Unsuffixed tags remain the full
  compatibility image. The images now run Python 3.13, with identical results on CPU, Intel GPU and
  Intel NPU. The Intel image is about 26 MB larger and decodes motion recordings on the GPU when
  `/dev/dri` is passed through; without it, it decodes on the CPU and says so. See
  [Hardware Acceleration](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.0/docs/setup/hardware-acceleration.md)
  before choosing an optional CPU, Intel or CUDA image.

## Privacy, retention and public access

- **Daily totals outlive your retention period.** They hold one count of captures and visits per
  species per day, with no photo, clip, time of day or camera. Guests never see a day that began
  before their own history window. **Delete all history** deletes the totals too, and its
  confirmation says so.
- **Garden may keep one more photo.** A domestic bird in Garden can keep YA-WAMF's own copy of the
  frame it was classified from. Like other kept Garden media, it counts towards your storage limit,
  shows in **Space used**, and ages out with your media retention.
- **A clearer delete choice for missing Frigate events.** The policy is now labelled
  **Delete records YA-WAMF has no photo for**. A record whose photo YA-WAMF still holds is marked
  missing instead of deleted, so the media integrity scan can no longer remove the only photo left.
- **Your Frigate address stays private.** When Frigate cannot be reached, guests see "Could not reach
  Frigate" with no address at all. As the owner you see only `scheme://host:port`, and logs drop
  user names, passwords and tokens from addresses.
- **About lists what really leaves your network.** It is built from when each request is made, says
  what each one sends, and now includes Google Fonts, Wikipedia and map tiles. Guests see requests
  that depend on owner settings marked **Owner only**.
- **The visit gap applies to guests as well**, so shared pages count visits the same way you do.

## Good to know

- **Health dismissals are kept in memory.** A restart, or **Clear error history**, brings dismissed
  warnings back with the rest of the error history.
- **The light level is only recorded from now on.** Older detections have no reading, and changing
  the sensor later leaves recorded readings as they are.
- **Totals for days before the update may be low.** A day that started before you updated may
  already have lost captures to an earlier cleanup, so its charts say it may be low.
- **Safari was checked in a browser engine, not on an iPhone.** The Safari fixes were tested in
  WebKit on Linux, which covers the layout but is not the same as a physical iPhone or Mac.
- **Not in this release.** Running several models together
  ([#804](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/804)) and feeder refill
  markers ([#946](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/946)) are still
  being reviewed.
