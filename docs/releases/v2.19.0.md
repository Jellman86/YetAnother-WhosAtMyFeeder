If you share your feeder with other people — a public link, a curious household — this release is
mostly for you: you now choose what visitors see, and your location stops being part of the deal.
For everyone, the dashboard gains a review queue that catches implausible first sightings, and the
weather on each visit can come from your own Home Assistant instead of a regional forecast.

## What's new

- **You choose what visitors see, per medium.** Under *Settings → Security*, with public access
  on: *Share audio*, *Share photographs*, and *Share video* are now separate switches. Audio
  previously had no control at all. Everything is enforced at the server, and a visitor is told a
  medium is not shared rather than shown an error.
- **A new species asks for one confirmation.** A first-ever sighting — the improbable rarity at a
  34% score — now appears in the dashboard's *Needs your call* queue as "New species · 1
  sighting". One dialog offers the three honest answers: confirm it as real, correct it to what it
  actually was, or block the species outright.
- **Weather from your own Home Assistant.** *Settings → Integrations → Home Assistant weather*
  takes your instance's URL, a long-lived access token, and a weather entity; optional per-reading
  overrides let a real outdoor sensor outrank the entity for temperature, wind, cloud, or rain. A
  sensor that cannot answer records *unknown* — never a forecast dressed up as a measurement — and
  every visit keeps the weather it was recorded with.
- **Custom Frigate labels.** If your Frigate model emits `duck` or `goose` as its own class,
  *Settings → Connection → Frigate labels to act on* now admits them. The list is for labels your
  model uses for birds — everything on it flows through the bird identification pipeline.
- **Notable nearby is clickable.** A rare eBird report near you opens the full species card —
  description, taxonomy, range — even for a bird your cameras have never recorded.
- **First-run setup can be skipped**, with an honest note about running without a password until
  you set one.

## Smoother everyday use

- **Filtering by a rare species is instant.** It used to read almost your whole history before
  answering; it now answers by index seek whether the species has three sightings or three
  thousand.
- **The lower dashboard can no longer be taken down by a data blemish.** A species arriving twice
  in the daily summary crashed everything below Top Visitors; the summary now merges such rows and
  the list survives them regardless.
- **The weather is stated once** in the visit view — one condensed line of measured facts, no
  disclosure hiding single numbers.
- **Delete says what it does.** "Delete this whole visit? The record and its media leave the
  history permanently. Hiding it instead keeps the record."
- **Workers survive big models.** A worker loading a large model on slow hardware was mistaken for
  dead and killed in a loop, which its owner saw as detections simply stopping. Loading now gets a
  proper warm-up window, activity on any channel counts as life, and if isolated workers still
  fail, classification continues in the main process with an amber notice instead of silence.
- **Memory stays flat.** The main process no longer hoards freed memory (measured: ~340 MB where
  3.2 GB had accumulated), video clips stream to disk instead of passing through its heap, and the
  OpenVINO compile cache persists so a slow first GPU compile is paid once.

## Before you update

- **No special steps.** One database migration runs automatically (new indexes only, fully
  reversible).

## Good to know

- **Changed default: visitors now see an approximate location.** If your instance is shared
  publicly, guest-facing features (like nearby-sightings searches) use your location rounded to
  about 10 km from this release on — an instance that previously exposed exact coordinates
  becomes coarser on upgrade, deliberately. Owners always see the exact position, and
  *Settings → Security → Location shown to visitors* restores *Exact* if that is what you want.
- The species filter's speed fix and the worker hardening both came from precise reports by users
  of this app. If something looks wrong at your feeder, an issue with what you measured is the
  most valuable thing you can send.
