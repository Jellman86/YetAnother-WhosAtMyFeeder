YA-WAMF 2.24.2 focuses **Needs your call** on uncertain birds in multi-bird captures, fixes hiding
BirdNET-Go audio calls, adds bulk management and search to **Audio History**, and cleans up the
**Health** page on phones.

## What's new

- **Needs your call leads with the uncertain bird.** On multi-bird captures, clicking thumbnails
  in the strip now previews them in the main viewer without overwriting your saved photograph
  (photo curation stays in **Open full record**). Wide full-frame captures with one uncertain bird
  zoom in on that bird right away with a **Show full photo** toggle, confident crops and counted
  birds of other species are tucked behind expandable **Show** controls, and naming an untied
  capture with a single unknown bird updates that counted bird too.
- **Common names on frame reads and counted birds.** Frame reads and counted birds now resolve
  model labels and taxonomic synonyms (such as `Corvus monedula` to **Western Jackdaw**) offline
  through the species catalogue and follow your common or scientific name preference.
- **Manage and search Audio History faster.** The per-row hide control is now a compact
  eye-with-slash icon (switching to an open eye to restore). Owners also get a **Hidden** view
  with a hidden count, **Multi-select** for bulk hiding and restoring with undo, and a live
  **Search** box across common names, scientific names, and audio sources.

## Smoother everyday use

- **Hiding BirdNET-Go audio calls works again.** Fixed a missing JSON `Content-Type` header on
  `PATCH /api/audio/history/{id}` that caused hiding a call to fail with a validation error
  ([#1075](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/issues/1075)).
- **Clearer Health incidents and mobile layout.** On phones, the **System status** badge now sits
  beside its heading so incident details use the full card width. Open incidents display their
  occurrence count, latest time, and camera, routine Frigate missing-media policy actions no
  longer mark System status as **Degraded**, and plain-language descriptions now cover backend
  diagnostic codes across all 9 languages.
- **Updated dependencies.** Refreshed backend Python, UI, telemetry-worker, and workflow
  dependencies.

## Before you update

- **No special steps.** This release has no database migrations and requires no configuration
  changes. Pull the new image and restart the container as usual.
- **Keep your image family unless you mean to change it.** Unsuffixed tags remain the full
  compatibility image; see
  [Hardware Acceleration](https://github.com/Jellman86/YetAnother-WhosAtMyFeeder/blob/v2.24.2/docs/setup/hardware-acceleration.md)
  for optional CPU, Intel and CUDA image tags.
